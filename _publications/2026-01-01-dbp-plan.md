**DBP-Plan: A Driving-Experience Layer for Reference-Line Generation at Urban Intersections**

*IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (T-ITS) | SCI Q1*

## Abstract

Urban intersections are the places where lane markings disappear and reference-line generation has to rely almost entirely on prior experience. This paper proposes DBP-Plan, a driving-experience layer that converts crowdsourced trajectories into a behaviour prior for intersection reference-line generation. Trajectories are partitioned by origin-destination topological relationships into human driving experience corridors, and trajectory support is decoupled from directional consistency so that empirical behaviour is characterised by a coherence-aware anisotropic tensor field. The tensor field is then turned into arc-length-integrated edge costs that guide node expansion and analytical goal connection in Hybrid A*, making the search converge toward human-like driving corridors between topological poses and yielding smooth, continuous reference lines after post-processing.
